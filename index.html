About INNOVENNEX

Built in Sabah, for Sabah.

INNOVENNEX is an aspiring Sabah-based company established on 20 January 2025, committed to delivering innovative solutions in ICT, engineering, fire safety, cleaning, and multidisciplinary services. We leverage hands-on experience in technical support, computer systems, and engineering projects to build a strong foundation in project execution and service delivery.

Guided by a culture of continuous improvement, we're positioning ourselves as a forward-looking technology and services provider that contributes to Malaysia's digital transformation and Sabah's economic growth.

01

Our Vision

To become a leading and trusted multi-disciplinary service provider in Malaysia, focused on technology development, infrastructure sustainability, and community well-being.

02

Our Mission

Deliver high-quality, safety-compliant services across engineering, ICT, cleaning and fire safety — while supporting local employment and entrepreneurship.

03

Our Goal

Provide consistent, committed project execution that contributes to Sabah's economic growth and creates local employment opportunities.

Accreditations

INNOVENNEX is a registered and accredited operator, qualified to supply, contract, and deliver services to both government and private clients across Malaysia.

MOF Registered

Registered with the Ministry of Finance Malaysia as a certified supplier of goods and services.

CIDB Registered (G1)

Registered with the Construction Industry Development Board under Grade G1, Categories B & CE.

PUKONSA Registered

Registered with Sabah's state contractor registry for both Supply & Services and Works Contractor categories.

Also recognised under the Sabah Bumiputera Business Certificate, with active business licences covering ICT, engineering, fire safety and cleaning. Full registration numbers and documentation are available on request.
